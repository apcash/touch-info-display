Home Touch Display

Home Touch Display, also labeled “at a glance,” is a personal household dashboard maintained by apcash. It combines a DAKboard display, outdoor weather, daily Bible passages, a checklist, Wi-Fi connection details, and signed-in access to printer status.

An optional Google Nest connection is being developed to display the temperature measured at the owner’s thermostat. This is a personal household project, not a public thermostat-control service.

The public site contains the app code. Household settings are stored locally on the display device; private printer status is retrieved through a signed-in account.
